ABOUT SHRI PURNA CHIKANS

Rooted in Lucknow.
Focused on chikankari.

Established in 1994, Shri Purna Chikans is rooted in Lucknow, Uttar Pradesh—the home of the celebrated Chikankari tradition. We offer a wide range of beautifully crafted Chikankari suits, kurtas and sarees, with collections for both wholesale and retail customers.

Our focus is on quality, elegant designs, traditional craftsmanship and customer satisfaction. We also aim to help aspiring and growing businesses build their offering with a manageable starting investment. Contact us to discuss current collections, minimum order quantities and wholesale terms.
